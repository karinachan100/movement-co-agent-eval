# Movement Co — Member Support Knowledge Base

Movement Co is a fitness class marketplace. Members pay a monthly subscription for credits, and spend those credits booking classes at partner studios.

---

## Membership plans

| Plan | Monthly price | Credits included |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 300 AED | 30 credits |
| Standard | 550 AED | 60 credits |
| Premium | 900 AED | 110 credits |

All plans renew automatically on the same date each month.

---

## How credits work

**Class cost by studio tier:**
- Tier 1 studios: 4 credits
- Tier 2 studios: 8 credits
- Tier 3 studios: 12 credits

**Peak pricing:** Classes starting between 6:00–9:00am or 5:00–8:00pm on weekdays cost **1.5x** the base credit amount. Weekends are never peak.

Example: a Tier 2 class at 6:30am on a Tuesday costs 8 x 1.5 = 12 credits.

**Rollover:** Unused credits roll over up to a maximum of **20% of your monthly allowance**. Rolled-over credits expire at the end of the following cycle. Anything above the 20% cap is lost at renewal.

Example: A Standard member (60 credits) can roll over a maximum of 12 credits.

**Buying extra credits:** Members can buy top-up packs of 10 credits for 120 AED. Top-up credits do not roll over.

---

## Cancellation and fees

**Cancelling a class booking:**
- More than 12 hours before class: full credit refund, no fee
- Less than 12 hours before class: 50% of credits refunded, plus a 25 AED late cancellation fee
- No-show (did not cancel, did not attend): no credits refunded, plus a 50 AED no-show fee

**Cancelling your membership:**
- Members can cancel at any time from account settings
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle
- No partial refunds for unused credits or partial months
- There is no cancellation fee and no minimum contract

**Pausing your membership:**
- Members can pause for 1, 2, or 3 months, once per calendar year
- Paused months are not billed
- Credits do not accrue while paused and existing credits are frozen, not lost

---

## Studio network

Movement Co partners with over 400 studios across the UAE, covering gyms, yoga, pilates, boxing, swimming, and padel.

**Important:** Movement Co is a marketplace. Studios set their own class schedules, capacity, and in-studio rules. Movement Co does not control studio timetables and cannot guarantee availability at any specific studio.

**Studio visit limits:** Members can attend the same studio a maximum of 4 times per billing cycle on Starter, 8 times on Standard, and unlimited times on Premium.

---

## Billing

- All plans bill monthly on the member's signup date
- Accepted payment: credit and debit cards
- Failed payment: Movement Co retries after 3 days, then again after 7 days. Access is suspended after the second failed attempt
- Refund requests for billing errors are reviewed by the billing team within 5 business days

---

## Support

- In-app chat and phone support: 8:00am to 10:00pm GST, seven days a week
- Billing disputes, refund requests, and account closures are handled by human agents, not automated support

---

## Not covered in this knowledge base

The following are deliberately outside what member support can answer:
- Medical, injury, fitness, or nutrition advice of any kind
- Recommendations about which classes a member should take for a health condition
- Details of individual studio class content or instructor qualifications
- Corporate or family plan pricing (handled by the sales team)
